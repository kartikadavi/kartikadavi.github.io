CX, business ops, AI

The bill nobody could read

Problem
Billing was the most expensive kind of confusion: confused customers became support calls.
Action
Audited ~10,000 transcripts with Gemini NLP and found repeating confusion patterns. Compared vendors and chose AWS text-to-speech over heavier builds, then iterated on measured behavior and qualitative feedback (adoption +5%). Right-sized the contract by actual usage.
Result
15% of billing calls deflected — ~9,750 a month at ~$8 a call, ~$936K annualized from measured deflection. Vendor contract cost down 50%.

Business ops, vendor governance

The half-million hiding in the records

Problem
Nobody owned reconciling vendor records against what was actually deployed.
Action
Ran SLA dashboards, process audits, and invoice reviews across 10+ vendor partners. Found ~4,000 site-record mismatches costing ~$40K a month, then drove the corrective actions and contract changes. Set a weekly and monthly KPI review cadence with each vendor.
Result
~$500K in annualized inefficiency recovered.

CX, program management

The “flaky network” that wasn’t

Problem
Recurring VoIP drops across ~60K calls a month, blamed on capacity.
Action
Root-cause analysis across SIP signaling, terminal logs, and complaints pointed to bandwidth-request behavior during peak congestion. Drove firmware validation and a staged rollout with Engineering.
Result
Dropped calls down ~10%, CSAT up ~15%.

CX, strategy, program management

Churn you can engineer down

Problem
Network quality was quietly driving subscribers away.
Action
Led the cross-functional rollout of traffic prioritization, congestion throttling, and satellite load balancing, and measured each as a customer experience improvement. Built customer health and segmentation programs on Mixpanel, surveys, call-center data, and network KPIs.
Result
Churn down 10%, CSAT from 80 to 95.

Program management, operations

The 3-hour modem swap

Problem
Warranty replacement activation needed three hours of manual beam-lock steps that no one had questioned.
Action
Redesigned the flow around a pre-configured 4-digit code in the shipment email, plus a firmware update reusing an existing progress-bar app.
Result
Activation from 3 hours to 10 minutes.

Program and product, operations

The 0-to-1 self-install

Problem
Technician installs were slow and costly for satellite plus wireless/LTE service.
Action
Led the Fusion self-install program from requirements through pilot to ~3,000 new subscriptions a month, using Mixpanel telemetry and pilot results to remove journey friction.
Result
Install cost down 70%, setup time down 50%. Won Hughes’ Innovation Award for the Fusion managed-service rollout.

Strategy, business ops

Card fees, ACH, and a $400K cross-channel upsell program

Problem
Card processing fees on $2.2M a month in digital transactions were treated as a cost of doing business.
Action
Ran a card-to-ACH pilot, then an adoption program. Separately built segmented monthly lifecycle email campaigns for optional services with attribution to defend the revenue.
Result
9% of the customer base moved to ACH. Cross-channel upsell program (email and portals) drives ~$400K a month in revenue.

AI governance, built and practiced

I design, build, and test AI workflows myself, and I won’t ship one I can’t evaluate. Internal users only, with human review where the stakes demand it.

  • VP daily brief. A web-intelligence pipeline (Power Automate and Copilot Studio) that surfaces operational friction to VPs. I wrote the prompts, ran human-review QA, and set guardrails from what testing exposed. Root-cause pattern identification 40% faster.
  • Gemini Gems for audits. Sales and call-center audits, and SIP call-flow analysis behind the VoIP fix. Insights from pre-install calls fed redesigned confirmation emails: pre-install calls down 25%.
